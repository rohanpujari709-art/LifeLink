About LifeLink

LifeLink is a Smart Blood Donor Management and Emergency Response System that improves how potential blood donors are found in normal and emergency situations. It gives patients, donors, hospitals and blood banks one central interface.

Traditional methods rely on phone calls, social media posts and separate records, which makes it hard to find suitable donors quickly. LifeLink offers donor registration, blood group search, location-based discovery, availability information and emergency request management.

Our Mission

To support timely blood donor discovery and improve coordination between donors, patients, hospitals and blood banks through an accessible digital platform.

Our Vision

A reliable, scalable platform that supports blood donation awareness, organised donor information and emergency response coordination.

Objectives

  1. Create a centralised donor management platform.
  2. Search potential donors by blood group.
  3. Support location-based discovery.
  4. Let donors register and update availability.
  5. Provide a structured emergency request process.
  6. Improve communication among users.
  7. Protect donor and patient information.
  8. Lay a foundation for hospital and blood bank integration.

Privacy and Data Protection

LifeLink supports blood donor discovery and emergency request coordination. It may process names, blood groups, contact details, locations, availability and request details.

  1. Data minimisation: collect only what the platform needs.
  2. User consent: obtain consent before collecting or sharing personal information.
  3. Access control: restrict sensitive records to authorised users.
  4. Location privacy: ask permission before using device location and never show exact private addresses.
  5. Secure authentication: use proper authentication and access controls.
  6. Data accuracy: let users correct outdated information.
  7. Data protection: protect data in storage and transit.
  8. Limited disclosure: share contact details only under platform rules and applicable law.
